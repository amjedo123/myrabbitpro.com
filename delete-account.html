Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your RabbitPro account and the data associated with it. RabbitPro is developed by NAS Albeit Rabbitry.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

Steps

  1. Open the RabbitPro app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email support@myrabbitpro.com from the address on your account and include the subject line "Delete my account." Include your account email in the body if it differs from the sending address. We'll verify the request and delete your account within 30 days, and will confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account credentials (email and password)
  • Your profile information (name, farm/rabbitry name)
  • All rabbitry data you've entered — rabbits, breedings, litters, health records, weights, feed logs, and financial records
  • Any for-sale listings you've published

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is retained, and for how long

A small amount of information is kept after deletion, separate from your app account:

  • Subscription and purchase history: if you've subscribed to a paid plan, records of that transaction are retained by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat under their own retention policies (for billing, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes) — we do not control this data once it leaves our systems. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Crash and diagnostic logs: anonymized technical logs already captured by Firebase Crashlytics before your deletion request may persist briefly in that system's own logs, but are not linked back to your account or rabbitry data and contain no personal information.
  • Buyer inquiries you submitted on someone else's listing: if you contacted a breeder through their published listing, that inquiry is retained by the breeder (not us) until they act on it, per our Privacy Policy.

Everything else described above under "What gets deleted" is removed in full — nothing else is retained.

Questions

If you have questions about this process, contact us at support@myrabbitpro.com.
